1. The Tri-Shield Healer (HSA — Health Savings Account)
Concept: A legendary battle-medic clad in radiant, triple-layered armor. While most warriors take damage at the start, middle, or end of a campaign, the Tri-Shield Healer deflects all three.
• Superpower: The Triple-Ward Shield
	• Deflects physical, chemical, and hallucinogenic attacks
• Special Skills:
	• Elder’s Awakening: Once the medic reaches Level 65, he gains attack ability
	• Time Travel: Can attack players in the past to destroy them in the future
• Limitations:
	• Healer’s Oath: Can only heal in his youth, not attack
	• Heavy Armor Requirement: Armor can only be bought by fording the treacherous Jade river
• Rank: 100

2. The Crimson Phoenix (Roth 401k) [use previous image iteration to avoid copyright issue]
Concept: A heavy-armored knight belonging to the Order of the Roth. He pays a heavy toll to forge his weapons, which grant him complete immunity to dragons later.
• Superpower: Dragon Immunity
	• No health, achievements, or booty can ever be lost to dragons
• Special Skills:
	• Guild Patronage: his local Guild automatically summons allied mercenaries to fight alongside him for free
	• Heavy Arsenal: Can carry massive amounts of gold into battle each year—far more than their lighter Rogue counterpart.
• Limitations:
	• Guild-Locked: Can only be recruited if his local guild offers a barracks for him
	• Upfront Toll: Weapons may only be forged with payment radiant opal 
• Rank: 95

3. The Shadow Rogue (Roth IRA)
Concept: A nimble, independent operative from the same Order of the Roth as the Crimson Phoenix. While he cannot carry as much heavy treasure into battle, he owns no allegience beyond his ages-past commission in the king's army. He belongs to no Guild and can slip out of danger at a moment's notice.
• Superpower: Free Agent
	• After breaking from the king's ranks, he pays no dues and follows no orders
• Special Skills:
	• Royal Pilfering: His knowledge of the royal fortress allows him to plunder the king's treasure, even before he gains the ability to morph at at Level 59.5.
	• Solo Operative: Requires no Guild sponsorship; any adventurer with earned gold can hire them.
• Limitations:
	• Light Pack: Restricted to carrying a modest cap of gold per year ($7,500).
	• Upfront Toll: Can only be hired by precious sapphire mined in one's youth
• Rank: 90

4. Attack Advisor (529 Plan)
Concept: A shrewd counselor who plans the king's attacks and advises his campaigns
• Superpower: Academy Protection
	• The advisor cannot be attacked from within the safety of the Royal Academy
• Special Skills:
	• Transmutation: Can bestow the academy endowment upon the Emerald Rogue or other warrior seeking a better life experience
	• Scholarship Transfer: If one of his students finishes their training early, he can reassign the student's scholarship to another warrior
	• Regional Blessing: Blesses warriors from his home region with springtime gifts
• Limitations:
	• Scholar's Vow: He is strictly confined to the Royal Academies
• Rank: 85

5. The Electric Storm (Traditional 401(k))
Concept: An frontliner who enters battle with a powered-up shield that deflects early enemy strikes, though his defenses later fizzle.
• Superpower: Electrified Shield
	• Projects a massive energy barrier at the start of battle that blocks attacks and knocks down enemies
• Special Skills:
	• Ally Reinforcements: Guild leaders endow his rare shield with extra power
	• Colossal War Chest: Carries massive gold into battle each year, dwarfing the carrying capacity of lone wandering warriors.
	• Unbound Prestige: Welcomes even the wealthiest lords to his forces without turning them away.
• Limitations:
	• Guild Exclusive: Can only be summoned if your commanding lord grants access to his barracks.
	• Power Drain: His shield loses all its power and becomes a heavy liability to carry
	• Desertion Curse: Attempting to discharge before level 59½ destroys his health
	• Escaped Electric: Once he hits level 73, he can no longer restrain his voltage and it flashes out of control
• Rank: 80

6. The Dawnbreaker (Traditional IRA)
Concept: A fierce frontline fighter who quickly destroys enemy fighters at the start of battle, but lacks endurance and sustains heavy injuries as the battle progresses.
• Superpower: Instant Fury
	• Wipes out entire sections of the attacking armies at the outset of a battle
• Special Skills:
	• Special Quests: Skilled at carrying out special quests without taking any damage 
• Limitations:
	• The Final Reckoning: Mid to late battle, his strength melts and he endangers himself and the king's army
	• Forced March: Game rules force this warrior to march out and spend his gold to fortify the king's troops whether there is a need or not
• Rank: 75

7. The Nimble Ranger (ABLE Account)
Concept: A swift pathfinder built for autonomy and survival in rugged terrain. Supplies warriors who would have to wait months for the king's official convoy. Free to provide bounty for living well right now without losing her stipend and blessing from the king.
• Superpower: Stealth Supply Line
	• Keeps gold out of convoy ambush attacks so the army never loses essential rations
• Special Skills:
	• King's Blessing: Owes no tax to the king on gold withdrawn from the offical supply chest
	• Pathfinder’s Versatility: Helps the troops obtain any item or help that improves quality of life
	• High Cap & Heavy Quiver: The king allows her to store up ample supply each year, which can be used freely at any age
• Limitations:
	• Final Tribute: When the ranger falls at the end of the game, the king takes back any booty gained through war
  • Only warriors approved by the king may receive help from the ranger
• Rank: 85
